# FIFA World Cup Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
A three-page Power BI dashboard analysing FIFA Men's World Cup history, team performance and match outcomes.

## Business Questions
- How have tournament participation and scoring changed over time?
- Which teams have the strongest historical win rates and goal differences?
- How do match outcomes vary by tournament stage?
- How do title-winning campaigns compare with other team-match records?

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Data visualisation and exploratory analysis

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Tournament Overview (1930–2022):** World Cup editions, title counts, host countries, goals per match and participating teams.
2. **Match and Team Analysis (1930–2018):** Win percentage, goal difference, team attacking and defensive performance, and outcomes by tournament stage.
3. **Key Findings (1930–2018):** Goals scored and conceded per match, match results, knockout outcomes and title-winning campaign observations.

## Methodology
- Match-level and tournament-level data were analysed using separate coverage periods.
- Team-match records were used to calculate team-level statistics.
- Penalty-shootout winners were classified as winners and their opponents as losers.
- Historical match classifications and derived measures were checked during validation.

## Key Findings
- Brazil leads the title ranking with five championships through 2022.
- The 1954 tournament had the highest goals-per-match rate in the analysed editions.
- Seventeen of 21 title campaigns from 1930–2018 were unbeaten.
- Knockout match outcomes were analysed separately from group-stage outcomes.

## Limitations
This is a historical descriptive analysis. Differences between champions and other teams do not establish causation. Historical team names and tournament formats may differ across editions.
